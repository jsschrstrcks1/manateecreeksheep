# Pipeline v4 — 8-Pen Linear Advancement Flow

**Date:** 2026-04-22
**Supersedes:** Pipeline v3 (7-pen closed loop). v3 never matched ground truth — stages 2/3/4/5 rams didn't match design. v4 is built from what's actually on the farm.
**Purpose:** A single-entry linear pipeline where ewes enter at one pen, advance through 7 more stages over their productive lifetime, get culled if they fail a stage, and only the proven breeders reach the elite pen. Rams stay put at their stage; ewes advance.

---

## The core shape

**1 intake pen + 7 breeding stages = 8 pens total.**

```
Goose Pen ──► Pen 3 ──► Tree Fort ──► Pen 4 ──► Pen 5 ──► Pen 6 ──► Pen 1 ──► Pen 2
(QUARANTINE) (Stage 1) (Stage 2) (Stage 3) (Stage 4) (Stage 5) (Stage 6) (Stage 7/ELITE)
```

- **Goose Pen** is intake/quarantine for purchased animals only (not for home-born lambs). An animal bought elsewhere enters here, clears health screening, then goes straight to whichever breeding stage fits its age and proven record.
- Home-born lambs enter the pipeline at **Pen 3 (Stage 1)** after weaning with their dam.
- Ewes advance one stage per year if they meet the annual-eval criteria for that stage. Fail criteria = cull or demote.
- Rams are fixed to their stage. One ram per pen.
- **Proximity note:** if your actual farm geography doesn't put these in walking order, renumber — the stage names are less important than keeping the flow moving one gate at a time. Tell me the actual adjacency and I'll remap.

## Stage definitions

| Stage | Pen | Role | Selection pressure (vs. previous stage) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Goose Pen | **QUARANTINE — purchased animals only**, 30-day hold | Zero; observation only. Health screening. |
| 1 | Pen 3 | **INTAKE** for weaned home-born ewe lambs; first breeding exposure | Minimal. Everyone gets a chance. |
| 2 | Tree Fort | **GROW-OUT / first annual eval** | Passed Stage 1: conceived, lambed, raised lamb to weaning, FAMACHA ≥2 through the year |
| 3 | Pen 4 | **PRODUCTION** | Passed Stage 2: 0 perinatal loss, lamb weaning weight ≥ flock median, no FAMACHA 4+ events |
| 4 | Pen 5 | **PROVEN** | Passed Stage 3: twin or better, lamb weaning ≥ flock 60th pct, FAMACHA ≤2 average |
| 5 | Pen 6 | **ADVANCED** | Passed Stage 4: twin+ at least one year, no parasite events requiring treatment |
| 6 | Pen 1 | **PRE-ELITE** | Passed Stage 5: lifetime FAMACHA average ≤2, no dewormer treatments in 2 consecutive years, lamb weaning ≥ flock 75th pct |
| 7 | Pen 2 | **ELITE / preservation** | Passed Stage 6: top quartile on EVERY metric; only 3-5 ewes at a time; bred to the best ram we have |

## Ewe flow — how advancement actually works

**Annual advancement** at the same time every year (suggest: November, post-weaning, pre-breeding). Every ewe gets reviewed against her current stage's exit criteria:

- **PASSES:** moves up one stage next breeding cycle
- **FAILS:** culled OR demoted to prior stage OR held in place pending one more chance (stage-dependent — stages 1-3 get a second chance; stages 4+ cull on first failure)

Some ewes stop advancing on purpose because they're performing well at their current stage. That's fine — you don't force an ewe out of Stage 4 just because she's been there 3 years, if she's producing quality lambs. What triggers movement is either passing criteria (advance) or failing (cull/demote).

**Lambs** stay with their dam in whichever pen they're born. At weaning:
- Ewe lambs → Pen 3 (Stage 1 intake)
- Ram lambs → Goose Pen grow-out if potential breeders, or culled/sold by 6mo if not

**Purchased animals:**
- Ram: Goose Pen 30-day quarantine with BSE + B. ovis + FEC, then placed in whatever stage fits (usually Stage 4 or higher for a proven ram)
- Ewe: Goose Pen 30-day quarantine, then placed in Stage 1 or 2 regardless of prior history (she has to earn advancement on our ground)

## What triggers promotion vs. demotion

### Promotion (ewe moves one stage up)
- **All stage criteria met** for the past 12 months
- **No parasite treatment** required in the past year
- **Lamb raised to weaning** without intervention
- **FAMACHA scoring** averages within stage threshold

### Holdback (ewe stays at current stage, no promotion)
- Met minimum but not excelled
- Had one parasite treatment but recovered cleanly
- Single when twinning was expected at her stage
- First-parity ewes typically stay one extra year at their current stage

### Demotion (ewe moves one stage down, one chance to recover)
- Parasite treatment >1 time in the year
- Lamb lost to preventable causes
- Lambing problems (dystocia, poor mothering)

### Cull (ewe leaves the flock)
- FAMACHA 4+ event (stages 4+) or 5 event (any stage)
- Open year (stages 3+)
- Second consecutive year of demotion criteria
- Chronic condition issues (e.g., G023-style)
- Conditional_cull flag triggers (e.g., Elsie Large White Triplet if her lambs fail)

### Ram rotation
Rams don't advance; they either stay, get replaced, or get culled. Ram annual review happens same time as ewe review:
- **Stage ram stays:** offspring meet stage criteria; FAMACHA clean; libido intact
- **Stage ram replaced:** one of the lambs coming up through the pipeline proves better, or a better purchased ram clears quarantine
- **Stage ram culled:** FAMACHA events; fertility issues; progeny parasite trend
